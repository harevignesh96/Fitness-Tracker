FRUITS — GERMAN SUPERMARKET LIST
●Bananas — your pre-workout fuel, cheapest energy in the store. Buy weekly.
●Apples — cheap all year, perfect mid-morning snack.
●Oranges / mandarins — vitamin C for recovery, great in winter.
●Blueberries & strawberries — buy fresh in summer; rest of the year grab frozen berries (TK-Beeren) — same nutrition, half the price, great in oats.
●Plums (Zwetschgen) — cheap and everywhere Aug–Sep.
●Aim for 2 fruits a day: banana around training + one other.

REST & RECOVERY RULES
●Between sets: 2–3 min heavy compounds, 60–90 sec isolation moves.
●Same muscle: at least 48 hrs before training it hard again.
●Sleep: 7–8 hrs — muscle is built in bed, not in the gym.
●Thursday: active rest (walk + stretch). Sunday: full rest, no guilt.
●Every 6–8 weeks: one easy week with lighter weights — your joints will thank you.
●Supplements: whey (optional, post-workout) · creatine 3–5 g daily, any time · check vitamin D with a doctor.
